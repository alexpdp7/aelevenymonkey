# First panel

Gabe and Tycho are sitting on a couch.
Gabe is sad and playing with a game controller.
Tycho speaks smugly with his eyes closed.

Gabe: Why did I buy Marvel Tokon?

Tycho: Because you believe you are **better than you are**.

# Second panel

Gabe is smiling and playing in an arcade, wearing a sheepskin jacket and sunglasses, against someone who is not looking very happy.
An onlooker seems scared of Gabe.

Tycho (off-screen): You're like an old man that buys a sports car he can't get in and out of.
Your heart aches for a time you can never return to - a time you squandered.
A time when you played a fighting game like a **violin**.

# Third panel

Gabe and Tycho continue in the couch, and they are side-eyeing each other.

Gabe: I got it 'cause I like Spider-Man!

Tycho: No. I'm right on this one.
